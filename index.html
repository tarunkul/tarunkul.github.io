01 / HOW I CAN HELP

Start with the problem, data, or workflow—not with a particular AI tool.

I help teams clarify what is feasible, build the right technical approach, and turn it into something that can be tested and used.

01

Documents are still being read, copied, or checked manually.

Turn documents into usable data and workflows

Transform PDFs, scans, forms, invoices, financial documents, tables, and mixed content into validated structured information, searchable knowledge, or controlled downstream workflows.

PDF / Scan→AI extraction→Validation→Excel / DB / API
02

Internal knowledge cannot be sent freely to public AI services.

Build AI around your private knowledge

Design private or local LLM and knowledge systems for source-aware answers, document understanding, structured generation, controlled assistants, and bounded agentic workflows.

Private data→Local AI / retrieval→Trusted answers
03

An image or sensing problem needs more than an off-the-shelf model.

Turn images and visual data into decisions

Develop custom computer-vision and deep-learning approaches for classification, detection, sensing, image analysis, generative vision, domain adaptation, and specialized visual problems.

Images / sensors→Vision model→Detection / insight
04

There is a promising AI idea, but the feasible path is unclear.

Find out what AI approach will actually work

Assess data, privacy, model choices, compute needs, evaluation, and implementation risk—then move from a research or business question to experiments and a working prototype.

Problem→Feasibility→Experiments→Prototype
