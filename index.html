01 / ABOUT

Research depth. Engineering mindset. Practical outcomes.

I hold a Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering from IIT Roorkee and bring over a decade of experience spanning AI research, software engineering, GPU/HPC experimentation, and applied AI development.

During my time at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University and UBDA, I collaborated with multidisciplinary research teams on ML/DL problem formulation, methodology design, model development, experimental evaluation, and compute planning. My research has contributed to publications at leading venues including CVPR, AAAI, WACV, and IEEE journals.

I currently focus on translating advanced AI methods into practical systems, particularly in document intelligence, private and local LLMs, generative AI, workflow automation, and efficient deployment across CPU and GPU environments.
